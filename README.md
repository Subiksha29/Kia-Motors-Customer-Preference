# Kia Motors – Customer Preference Study

## About the Project
This project focuses on studying consumer preference towards Kia Motors with special reference to Coimbatore District.

The study examines the factors influencing customers when choosing Kia cars and their opinions about the brand's performance.

## Objectives
- To identify the mode of car purchase by consumers.
- To study the factors influencing consumers in choosing cars.
- To assess customer opinions on Kia Motors' brand performance.
- To test the association with selected socio-economic variables.

## Research Methodology
- Research Design: Descriptive Research
- Study Area: Coimbatore District
- Sample Size: 180 Respondents
- Data Collection: Primary and Secondary Data
- Primary Data Tool: Questionnaire

## Statistical Tools
- Percentage Analysis
- Chi-Square Test
